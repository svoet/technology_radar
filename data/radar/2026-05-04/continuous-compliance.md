Continuous compliance is the ongoing, automated process of monitoring systems, infrastructure, and workflows to ensure they meet regulatory standards (like GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2) and internal security policies at all times, rather than just during periodic audits. It shifts from manual, reactive, point-in-time checks to real-time, automated "always-on" audit readiness.
Key Aspects of Continuous Compliance:
- Automation: Uses tools to continuously scan for vulnerabilities, configuration changes, and policy deviations, replacing manual, resource-intensive audits.
- Real-time Monitoring & Alerting: Instantly detects compliance gaps and alerts staff to fix risks immediately, preventing them from snowballing.
- Constant Audit Readiness: Provides continuous evidence collection, removing the burden of manual, pre-audit preparation.
- Proactive Risk Management: Addresses compliance issues early in the DevOps lifecycle.
