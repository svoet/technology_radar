IT sovereignty is the ability of an organization or nation to control its own digital destiny, ensuring data, software, and infrastructure are not subject to foreign laws, interference, or dependency on single external providers. It encompasses data residency, technical control, and operational independence, often focusing on cloud compliance and security.
Key components of IT sovereignty include:
- Data Sovereignty: The assurance that data resides within specific geographical borders and is subject only to the laws of that jurisdiction.
- Technological Sovereignty: The capacity to control the technology stack, often utilizing open-source or localized solutions to avoid lock-in by foreign vendors.
- Operational Sovereignty: Maintaining control over IT management and security, ensuring that local entities manage critical digital infrastructure. 
IT sovereignty acts as a strategic imperative to build resilient, compliant, and secure digital foundations aligned with local legal and economic goals
