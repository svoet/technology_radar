SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response) is a technology stack that enables security teams to integrate disparate tools, automate repetitive tasks, and execute standardized incident response workflows. By consolidating threat data and automating responses through playbooks, SOAR improves operational efficiency, reduces, and lowers mean time to response (MTTR).
Key Components of SOAR
- Security Orchestration: Connects and integrates internal and external tools (firewalls, SIEM, endpoint security) via APIs, allowing them to work together.
- Security Automation: Executes tasks without human intervention, such as enriching alerts, prioritizing events, and updating firewall rules.
- Security Response: Streamlines incident management using playbooks to guide analysts through standardized procedures.

Key Usage Examples
- Phishing Investigation: Automatically parsing suspicious emails, checking links/attachments, and deleting the email from user inboxes if malicious.
- Threat Intelligence Enrichment: Automatically checking indicators of compromise (IOCs) against threat intel feeds to determine threat severity.
- Endpoint Investigation & Response: Automatically isolating a ransomware-compromised laptop from the network.
- Vulnerability Management: Triaging incoming vulnerability alerts and assigning them to appropriate teams for remediation
