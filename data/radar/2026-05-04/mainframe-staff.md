Mainframe staffing in 2026 is characterized by a "critical paradox": high demand for expertise alongside shrinking, aging teams and flat budgets. While 91% of organizations plan to hire mainframe talent, many struggle to find personnel who can bridge legacy systems with modern hybrid, AI-enabled environments, leading to a reliance on "skeleton crews" for mission-critical operations.
