Network Detection and Response (NDR) is a cybersecurity solution that uses machine learning and behavioral analytics to monitor network traffic (both on-premise and cloud) in real time to detect, analyze, and automatically respond to suspicious activities. It provides comprehensive visibility, focusing on identifying threats like lateral movement and compromised credentials that evade signature-based defenses.
Key Aspects of NDR:
- Behavioral Modeling: Instead of just signatures, NDR establishes a baseline of "normal" behavior, flagging deviations that indicate a potential breach.
- Comprehensive Visibility: Monitors both North-South traffic (entering/leaving the network) and East-West traffic (moving internally).
- Automated Response: Acts immediately upon detecting threats, such as triggering firewalls to block malicious traffic, isolating infected devices, or terminating suspicious user sessions.
- Encrypted Traffic Analysis: Analyzes malicious behavior within encrypted traffic streams without requiring decryption.
