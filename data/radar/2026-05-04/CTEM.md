Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM) is a proactive, five-stage framework developed by Gartner to manage cybersecurity risks. It goes beyond traditional vulnerability scanning by continuously identifying, validating, and prioritizing threats based on actual exploitability. CTEM focuses on fixing the most critical issues to the business rather than all vulnerabilities equally.
Key Components and Features of CTEM
- Proactive Approach: Unlike reactive vulnerability management, CTEM helps organizations stay ahead by constantly assessing risks, according to.
- Beyond Vulnerabilities: CTEM covers misconfigurations, identity risks, and external attack surfaces.
- Business Alignment: It prioritizes fixes based on potential business impact and real-world exploitability, notes.
