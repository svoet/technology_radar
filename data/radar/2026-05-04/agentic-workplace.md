An Agentic Workplace Solution is the next evolution of enterprise AI, moving beyond "chatbots that talk" to "agents that do." In 2026, we’ve shifted from Generative AI (writing emails or summarizing docs) to Agentic AI, which is characterized by agency—the ability to plan, reason, use tools, and execute multi-step business processes autonomously within a set of guardrails.
