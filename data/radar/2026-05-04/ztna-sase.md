Universal Zero Trust (ZTNA) & SASE Maturity

The "Secure Access Service Edge" (SASE) has moved from a buzzword to the default architecture.

- Identity as the Perimeter: Networking and security have fully converged. Access is no longer granted based on "being on the office Wi-Fi." Instead, every single packet is verified based on user identity, device health, and "behavioral velocity" (e.g., "Is it physically possible for this user to be in London and then log in from Tokyo 10 minutes later?").
- Agentic Security: SASE providers are embedding AI agents that can "quarantine" a suspicious device and initiate a deep-scan without human intervention, reducing the Mean Time to Remediation (MTTR) from hours to seconds.
