The current geopolitical situation is significantly impacting enterprise IT by driving fragmentation of the global internet (splinternet), forcing stringent data localization, accelerating state-sponsored cyberattacks, and disrupting hardware supply chains. Companies are shifting from globalized IT to regionalized, secure, and resilient infrastructure to handle risks, increasing operational costs
