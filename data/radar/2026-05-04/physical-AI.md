Physical AI refers to AI systems that operate in, interact with, and understand the physical world, moving beyond software-only tools to control hardware via sensors and actuators. It enables machines like robots, autonomous vehicles, and drones to perceive surroundings and act autonomously. Key examples include AI-powered robotic arms, self-driving cars, delivery drones, and smart industrial systems.
