An IT Platform strategy is a business and technology framework that creates long-term value by facilitating interactions between users, partners, and systems, often creating network effects. It moves from isolated applications to shared, scalable digital foundations—like API-driven infrastructures, app stores, or marketplaces—that boost efficiency, speed up development, and enable ecosystem growth.
Key Components & Concepts
- Platform as a Product: Treating internal technology as a product designed to serve internal developers/business units, aimed at improving velocity and security.
- User-centric: designed around the user and the user experience rather than the technology
- Roadmap: Implements a global roadmap for all processes and tools included in the platform
- Governance: Sets rules for participation, security, and technical standards.
- Standardization & Reuse: Uses common technological foundations (APIs, shared services) to minimize duplicated effort and reduce complexity.
